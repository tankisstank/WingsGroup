# WingSide hi-fi travel assets

These files were copied from the approved Open Design project
`9f85d0db-9ebc-409a-b580-5b401e4a10b8/assets/hifi` on 2026-07-18 (refreshed 2026-07-19).

The product owner confirmed that these travel images are licensed for use in
the WingSide product. The images are intentionally kept out of generic shared
components; `scripts/sync-wingsside-hifi-assets.ts` scopes their upload and
page relationships to the `wings-side` site.

Public composition grids also serve copies from `public/wingsside-hifi/` for
stable inventory thumbs (`WingsSideHiFiSectionInventory`).
