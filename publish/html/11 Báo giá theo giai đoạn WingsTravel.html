Chi tiet giai doan 1

Day la phase duoc de xuat de chot bao gia o thoi diem hien tai

Trong boi canh hien tai, giai doan 1 nen duoc gop theo huong website da ngon ngu, CMS nen va campaign landing page. Cach nay giu duoc chat luong hien dien online, lead flow co ban va tinh san sang cho marketing ma chua can mo rong sang cac lop van hanh sau hon.

Giai doan 1

Website da ngon ngu + CMS nen + campaign landing page

Gia du kien 80 trieu

Day la phase xay nen hien dien online dau tien cho WingsTravel, voi trong tam giai doan dau la mang inbound: website v1 de gioi thieu dich vu, CMS nen de chu dong cap nhat noi dung, va khung landing page campaign co the nhan ban theo thi truong hoac nhom san pham.

Muc tieu

  • Co mot nen website du tot de ra thi truong.
  • Co the trien khai landing page theo campaign, thi truong hoac nhom san pham.
  • Chu dong quan tri noi dung thay vi sua tay roi rac.

KPI / tieu chi danh gia

  • Website v1 hoan thien va co the dung de gioi thieu dich vu bai ban.
  • CMS nen hoat dong on dinh de doi ngu chu dong cap nhat noi dung.
  • Co it nhat 1 khung campaign landing page co the tai su dung.
  • Form nhan lead va diem tiep nhan lead hoat dong on dinh.
  • Co the bat dau trien khai marketing ngay sau ban giao.

Pham vi du kien

  • Website gioi thieu dich vu va tour cho WingsTravel, voi trong tam giai doan dau la inbound.
  • Cau truc da ngon ngu giai doan dau.
  • Cac trang nen, form nhan lead va diem tiep nhan lead co ban.
  • CMS nen de quan ly bai viet, trang va noi dung.
  • Khung campaign landing page co the nhan ban.
Milestone 1

Lam ro cau truc website, ngon ngu uu tien, nhom noi dung va khung campaign landing page.

Milestone 2

Thiet ke va dung website v1 cung CMS nen.

Milestone 3

Hoan thien form nhan lead, luong tiep nhan lead co ban va khung landing page campaign.

Milestone 4

Review, chinh sua, ban giao va chot huong mo rong giai doan tiep theo.

Dau ra chinh

  • Website v1 da ngon ngu.
  • CMS nen de chu dong cap nhat noi dung.
  • Co kha nang tao landing page campaign theo thi truong hoac san pham.
  • Co nen lead flow ban dau cho marketing va sale.

Ghi chu pham vi

  • Muc nay phu hop khi khoa ro pham vi website v1 va mot khung landing page co the nhan ban.
  • Neu phat sinh them nhieu ngon ngu, nhieu loai campaign page, tich hop sau hoac logic tour phuc tap, se can boc bo sung.

Giai doan tiep theo

Cac phase sau can lam ro scope of work truoc khi boc bao gia

Sau khi giai doan 1 duoc xay xong va bat dau co dau vao thuc te, hai ben moi nen mo sang cac lop trong hon nhu lead, van hanh, du lieu va AI. O thoi diem hien tai, nhung phan nay chi nen giu o muc dinh huong, chua chot gia.

Ghi chu chien luoc Giai doan 2 tro di la nhung phase de mo rong sau khi website va marketing nen da co chuyen dong thuc te. O giai doan hien tai, phu hop nhat la chot gia cho phase 1, con cac phase sau scope roi moi bao gia.

Cach mo trao doi

Co the gui theo huong ro gia cho phase 1, nhung van mo cho roadmap dai hon

Phan nay dung de chot cach noi voi doi tac. Muc tieu la dua ra du gia tri de ra quyet dinh cho giai doan 1, nhung van giu duoc the cua doi tac cong nghe va huong mo rong sau khi scope cac phase tiep theo da ro.

Theo goc nhin cua em, de phu hop voi giai doan hien tai thi minh nen chia theo tung giai doan thay vi bao mot cuc chung. Giai doan 1 em de xuat gop luon phan website da ngon ngu, CMS nen va kha nang tao campaign landing page de ben anh co mot nen du tot cho viec gioi thieu dich vu, chay thi truong va nhan lead. Voi pham vi nay, em dang hinh dung muc ngan sach phu hop la khoang 80 trieu. Cac giai doan sau nhu quan ly lead, van hanh noi bo, du lieu hay AI thi em nghi nen lam ro them scope of work roi khi do em se boc tiep bao gia cu the cho tung phan.

Nhung gi chua bao gom

Ngan sach quang cao, mua domain, hosting, media lon, quay dung video, dich thuat so luong lon, va cac cong cu ben thu ba can license rieng.

Dieu kien de scope phase sau

Sau khi phase 1 chay xong hoac dau vao da ro hon, can lam ro quy trinh lead, van hanh, du lieu, KPI va cach phoi hop de scope va bao gia chinh xac cho cac phase tiep theo.
